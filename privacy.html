FocusPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

FocusPulse ("the App") is a screen-time manager and app blocker developed by AnskaitisGroup S.L. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Data collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on servers. There is no account system, no cloud sync, and no analytics or advertising SDKs included in the App.

2. On-device data

All information the App processes — including screen-time statistics, block schedules, and usage limits — is stored locally on your device only.

You can delete all App data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → FocusPulse → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Permissions

The App uses the following Android permissions, each with a specific purpose:

  • Usage Access — used to measure your screen time per app and enforce usage limits. This data never leaves your device.
  • Accessibility Service — used exclusively to detect when a blocked app is opened so the App can show the blocking screen, and to read page URLs for keyword-based website blocking. The App does not read other content.
  • Notification Access — used to suppress notifications from blocked apps during focus sessions. Notification contents are processed locally and are not stored or transmitted.
  • Device Admin — optional, used to prevent the App being uninstalled while Strict Mode is active.
  • Internet — not required for core functionality. Internet is only used if you opt in to optional features that require connectivity.

4. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
